# Impact of Video Games on Cognitive Abilities of Individuals

B.Tech. Project (BTP), DA-IICT. Mentor: Prof. Kalyan Sasidhar.
Group project with Abhimanyu Negi.

**Read the paper:** [PDF](video-games-cognitive-abilities-btp.pdf)

## Overview
A 7-week experiment with 30 participants across four experience levels
(no experience, beginner, amateur, professional) that measured how playing a
first-person shooter (Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare) affects reaction time
and mouse accuracy. The tests draw on Fitts' Law and Hick-Hyman Law.

## Method
- Phase 1: survey, grouping by experience, and a 1-week pilot
- Phase 2: two 30-minute sessions per week for 7 weeks, with tests before and after each session
- Analysis: box plots, skewed distributions and Bland-Altman analysis

## Key findings
- Reaction time dropped in every group, most for players with no experience (346 → 281 ms).
- Mouse accuracy rose in every group, again most for beginners and non-gamers.
- Professional players stayed within a narrow range across the weeks.

## Limitations
Small sample, similar ages, short duration and only 2 sessions per week.
